## Design Context

### Users
Children of primary-school age use Kidoo with a Fire TV remote to find and resume parent-approved videos. Parents curate the collection and configure profiles for different ages and languages.

### Brand Personality
Friendly, confident, playful. Kidoo should feel inviting to children and reassuring to parents without feeling babyish.

### Aesthetic Direction
Take cues from the familiar, artwork-led browsing of YouTube and Netflix while keeping navigation calm and legible from a sofa. Use Kidoo's existing logo, a warm off-white child palette, and the existing dark blue for grown-up areas; emphasize clear sections, large readable titles, and unmistakable remote focus. Colour signals context but never replaces authentication.

### Design Principles
1. Put a child's next action first; keep parent tools out of the way of browsing.
2. Organize movies, series, YouTube and family videos into recognizable places.
3. Present series as a single choice and reveal episodes only after selection.
4. Preserve approvals, each profile's progress and the distinction between content sources.
5. Make remote navigation predictable with clear focus, simple labels and useful empty states.
