# Kidoo individual contributor agreement — draft for review

**Draft:** This agreement is proposed for the project owner's and a lawyer's review. It has not been adopted, and submitting a contribution or opening a pull request does not indicate acceptance. It does not change the [Kidoo Community Licence](../LICENSE).

This draft is intended for contributions made by an individual. Contributions owned by an employer or another organisation need separate authorisation or an organisation-level agreement. The project has not yet established a signature or acceptance process.

## Parties and contributions

This agreement would be between Simone Andrea Pozzi, the Kidoo project owner ("Kidoo"), and the individual who signs it ("you"). A "Contribution" means a change to code, documentation, designs or other material that you intentionally submit to Kidoo for inclusion in the project, including subsequent revisions. A submission clearly marked as not intended for inclusion is excluded.

## Ownership and permission

You retain ownership of your Contributions. You grant Kidoo a worldwide, non-exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, transferable licence to use, reproduce, modify, prepare derivative works of, distribute, publicly display and perform, and sublicense each Contribution, in source or other form. Kidoo may exercise those rights in Kidoo Community, Kidoo Premium and other products or services, under open, source-available, commercial or other terms, and may permit others to do so.

This grant is not a transfer of copyright. You may continue to use and license your own Contributions elsewhere. Kidoo is not required to accept, use or publish a Contribution. This agreement does not change the licences already granted to recipients of Kidoo or grant rights in third-party material that you cannot license.

## Your assurances

For each Contribution you submit, you confirm that you created it and have the authority to grant the rights above, or that you have identified any third-party material and provided evidence that Kidoo may use it under compatible terms. If your employer or another person owns rights in a Contribution, obtain their written authorisation before submitting it. Do not submit confidential information, personal data or media without the necessary rights and permission.

You agree to identify material subject to other licences and include applicable notices. You are not expected to provide a warranty that the Contribution is error-free; you submit it as-is, to the extent permitted by law.

## Patents and trademarks

If you own or control patent claims necessarily infringed by your Contribution alone or by its combination with Kidoo as submitted, you grant Kidoo a worldwide, non-exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free licence to make, use, sell, offer for sale, import and otherwise transfer the Contribution and that combination, with the right to sublicense. This grant covers only claims you can license and does not grant rights to unrelated patents.

Neither party grants trademark rights under this agreement. Nothing in this agreement prevents either party from exercising rights that cannot lawfully be waived or limited.

## Proposed acceptance record

A final agreement should provide a reliable way to record the contributor's identity, the version accepted, the date, and the Contributions covered, with employer or other rights-holder authorisation where needed. The project owner should agree that process after legal review. Existing submissions are not retroactively covered merely because this draft exists.
